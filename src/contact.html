Getting Here

How to Find Us

By Tube

Take the District Line to Chiswick station. Exit and walk south along Chiswick High Road — we are approximately 2 minutes on the left.

By Bus

Routes 324 and 292 stop directly on Chiswick High Road outside the restaurant. Check TfL for live arrivals.

By Car

We are off the A5 Edgware Road. Free street parking is available on Chiswick High Road from 6 PM. Nearest postcode for sat-nav: W4 4AR.
