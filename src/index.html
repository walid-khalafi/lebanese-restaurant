Our Story & Culinary Heritage

Centuries of Lebanese Craft,
Served in London's Chiswick

The Tahdig story begins with our founder — a chef, a mother, and a keeper of ancient Lebanese recipes passed down through her family in Isfahan. Every dish at our Chiswick table carries a memory: the golden crunch of saffron rice pressed against a copper pot, the first aromatic steam of Ghormeh Sabzi on a winter morning, the warmth of real Lebanese hospitality that never asks if you've had enough.

Made Fresh Every Day From hummus to wraps to curries — everything is prepared fresh when you order. We source quality produce daily and cook from scratch, because that is what real Lebanese food actually is.
Real Charcoal Grilling Our shawarma and mixed grill are cooked over real charcoal — never a flat-top, never gas. The smoky char you get with every bite is the result of doing it properly, the way it should always be done.
Heritage of Chiswick Dining Since opening on Chiswick High Road, Nil Lebanese has become the local go-to — a neighbourhood table where families, workers and food lovers all eat well without breaking the bank.
