Before You Arrive

Booking Policies & FAQs

For parties of 6 or more we recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance. Groups of 10+ are very welcome — please call us on 020 4541 4261 to discuss arrangements. We'll do our best to accommodate your full party comfortably.

We ask for at least 24 hours notice for any cancellations or date changes. For groups of 8 or more, we kindly request 48 hours notice so we can offer the table to other guests. To amend or cancel a booking, please call us directly rather than using the online form.

Friday and Saturday lunchtimes and evenings are our busiest sessions — we recommend booking at least 2 days ahead. During UK public holidays we may adjust our hours slightly, so please call ahead on 020 4541 4261 to confirm.

All our meats are 100% Halal certified. We offer a wide range of vegetarian and vegan dishes clearly labelled on the menu. For serious allergies (nuts, gluten, dairy), please note them in the Special Requests field and inform your server on arrival so the kitchen can take extra precautions.

We love celebrating with you. Select your occasion in the form and we'll do our best to make it special — a candle, a complimentary dessert, or a personalised touch. For cake deliveries or decorations, just let us know at least 24 hours ahead.

We offer takeaway collection and local delivery for most menu items. Takeaway orders can be placed by calling 020 4541 4261. Note that some dishes — particularly our charcoal grills — are best eaten fresh in the café for the full experience. Still, most of our menu travels well and we do our best to pack it carefully.
