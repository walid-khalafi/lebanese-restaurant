Our Heritage

From Beirut's Tables
to Chiswick High Road

Nil Lebanese started with a simple idea: bring the honest, generous food of a Lebanese family kitchen to Chiswick. Not fine dining, not shortcuts — just fresh ingredients prepared properly, the way they were always meant to be eaten. Shawarma with real marinated meat. Hummus made from scratch. Mezze that tastes like it came from a home in Beirut.

Our kitchen on Chiswick High Road is open from 8 AM to 9 PM, seven days a week. Whether you come in for a full English at breakfast, a shawarma wrap at lunch, or a mixed grill in the evening — everything leaves the kitchen made to order, with quality you can taste. No frozen. No fuss. Just real cooking at prices that make sense.

Our Culinary Pillars

Made to Order, Every Time

Fresh Ingredients Daily

Nothing sits in a bain-marie waiting. Every wrap, every mezze platter, every curry is made fresh when you order it. We source quality produce daily and prep from scratch — because that's what Lebanese home cooking actually is.

No frozen, no shortcuts
100% Halal Prime Cuts

Natural Charcoal Grilling

Our shawarma, shish and mixed grill are cooked over real charcoal — never a flat-top grill, never a conveyor. The smoky char you get with every bite is not a flavouring; it's a result of doing it properly. All meat is 100% Halal certified British sourced.
